Brand Reflection Series · Episode 2 · companion to Color fingerprint & Sound fingerprint

Where Czech brands stand, and what's moving around them.

Homepage messaging from 23 major Czech brands embedded into a multilingual semantic space alongside 30 top rising Czech queries, projected to 2D. Dense clusters are red oceans. Isolated points are voices. Trends near empty zones are openings.

By Barbora Šandová. Source on GitHub.

Method

Brand homepage text (title, meta, headings, first paragraphs) extracted via trafilatura. Top rising Czech queries via pytrends with a hand-curated fallback when the unofficial API rate-limits. Both corpora embedded with intfloat/multilingual-e5-base (passage / query prefixes per model docs, L2-normalised). Layout via UMAP (n_neighbors=10, min_dist=0.3, cosine metric). Cluster labels are sector-rolled. Opportunity score = velocity × (1 − mean cosine-similarity to the 3 nearest brands), computed in the 768-dim embedding space, not on the 2D map.

03

Observations

01

Czech financial services has one voice.

All five banks in the corpus (ČSOB, Česká spořitelna, Fio, Air Bank, Komerční banka) plus Generali cluster as one. Incumbents and challengers are indistinguishable at the homepage layer. Air Bank's "different bank" positioning vanishes the moment the homepage opens. A new financial entrant doesn't need a better product to look distinctive; it needs a different homepage.

02

The mass-market consumer cluster is a templating problem.

Lidl, Albert, Rohlík, Staropramen, Škoda Auto, Pilulka and Seznam.cz sit in one cluster across five sectors. The shared shape is the homepage template: carousel of week's promos, "akce" headlines, product grid. The semantic distance between a discount grocer and an online pharmacy dissolves at the layer buyers actually see first.

03

Telecom's real competitive set is broader than telecom.

T-Mobile, O2 and Vodafone cluster with ČEZ (energy) and Avast (security). All five sell "always-on service" — uptime, reliability, bundled tariff language. To escape the cluster a telco has to break the always-on grammar, not out-feature the other two operators.

04

There is no clean strategic white space, only degrees of crowding.

Every trend in the corpus has a top-3-brand saturation above 0.74 — most sit at 0.80+. The least-crowded queries (voyo program, fotovoltaika dotace, vitamíny d3) are also the lowest-velocity. The classic "high momentum + low saturation" jackpot quadrant is empty here, which is itself a finding: Czech brand homepages have already absorbed the language of every trending topic.
